AKS Wealthply

A personal stock market scanner.

What it does

AKS Wealthply scans Indian stocks once a day and builds a daily briefing: market condition, strong sectors, and stocks that meet the scanner's filters. The results are saved as files in the owner's own Google Drive and shown on a private dashboard.

Why it asks for Google access

The app signs in to Google only to save and read its own result files in one Google Drive folder. It does not read email, contacts, or any other Google data.

Not investment advice

The scanner describes what is true in the data on a given day. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Trading involves risk, and decisions remain with the user.
