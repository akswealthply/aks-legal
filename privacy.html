Privacy Policy

AKS Wealthply · Last updated 17 November 2026

Who we are

AKS Wealthply is a personal stock market scanner run by its owner. This policy explains how the app handles Google account data.

What Google data the app accesses

The app uses Google sign-in (OAuth) only to create and read its own result files (daily scan reports and status files) in a single folder in the owner's Google Drive. It does not access email, contacts, calendar, photos, or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Drive access is used only to store the scanner's results and to load them into the dashboard. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties.

Storage and security

Sign-in credentials are kept private and are never published. The scan results contain market data, not personal information.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask about or delete any data, write to the address below.
